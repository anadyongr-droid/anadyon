## Why page 1 is not here

The scan supplied is a **completed contract for a real customer** — name, date of
birth, passport number, driving licence number and expiry, telephone, email, and
both signatures, all in handwriting on the front page.

That is not going into a git repository. History is permanent, and
`HANDOFF-H1.md` §7 is explicit about customer data.

Redaction was attempted and **abandoned as unverifiable**. The blank form is
printed in red-orange and the customer's entries are in red and blue pen, so no
colour rule separates handwriting from the form itself; a first attempt at
box-based redaction left the driver's name, the full licence number and the
notes line visible, and the check that was supposed to catch that had been
written to look *inside the boxes after painting them white* — which is
necessarily empty and therefore proves nothing. A second, stricter check on page
2 did fail honestly, and its two hits turned out to be a paper crease and the
page edge, confirmed by eye.

The front page's value was its **layout**, and the layout is reproduced in the
template. Nothing was lost by leaving the photograph out.

**If a front-page reference is wanted**, photograph a *blank* form. That has no
personal data in it at all and needs no redaction.
