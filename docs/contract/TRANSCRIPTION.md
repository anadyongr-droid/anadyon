carries the same eighteen articles in Greek alongside; **article 15 provides
that the Greek version prevails**, so the Greek column is the operative text and
this English transcription is a working reference, not the contract.

The scan is soft-focus and the right margin curls away on some lines. Where a
line ran off or could not be read with confidence it is marked `[…]`, rather
than guessed. Nothing below is paraphrased: where it is quoted, it is quoted.

**12.3.** In the case where the Lessee unilaterally gives notice of termination

fulfilled all his obligations stemming from this agreement, particularly those
mentioned in the previous article. **Any prepaid rent for the remaining period

**12.4.** This Agreement is terminated automatically and de jure in the event

**18. NOTIFICATIONS – ANNOUNCEMENTS.** Any notification or announcement with
