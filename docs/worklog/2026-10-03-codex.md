## Decided

- The guardian's authoritative path is deterministic policy and monitoring.
  AI may explain alerts but may not downgrade them, suppress them or remediate
  production.
- Keep Chrome access under Tasos's stated constraint, with turn-scoped approval,
  no persistent approval and an independent production watcher.
- Treat browser access to shared Supabase/Vercel origins as residual risk:
  origin controls cannot distinguish staging and production paths.
- Reuse the existing off-machine encrypted backup workflow as the deep
  production-check boundary.
- Use one shared policy with separate Claude Code and Codex adapters, because
  their hook and permission formats differ.
