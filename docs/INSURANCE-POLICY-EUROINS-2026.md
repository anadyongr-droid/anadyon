# What the Euroins policy actually says

**Read:** 2 September 2026, from the policy certificate Tasos supplied.
**Document:** Euroins Α.Ε. (Greek branch) policy no. **9190600829**, broker
K. Express Μ. ΕΠΕ (Zakynthos, reg. ΖΑΚ/93).
**Insured:** ΑΝΑΔΥΩΝ Ι.Κ.Ε, ΑΦΜ 800569811.
**Vehicle:** one — a 2010 Hyundai i20, plate **ΙΟΖ4176**.

---

## 0. What this document is, and what it is not

It is the three-page **policy certificate** (ασφαλιστήριο συμβόλαιο): the
schedule of who and what is covered, for how much, at what premium.

It is **not** the terms. §4.3 of the certificate says so explicitly — the
General Terms, the Special Terms and the Exclusions are in an attached booklet
(*Βιβλίο Όρων Ασφάλισης Αυτοκινήτου*) which was not part of the file supplied.
§2.1 and §2.2 then defer to it: exclusions are those set by Law 489/1976 **plus
"every additional exclusion contained in the relevant article of the attached
Special Terms."**

So the certificate can tell us what it *does* say. It cannot tell us what the
booklet says, and a driver-age exclusion — if one exists — would live in the
booklet. Everything below is scoped accordingly.

---

## 1. The question asked: are there age restrictions?

**Not in this document. There is no age condition of any kind in it.**

Searched the full extracted text for age words (ηλικία, ετών), for numeric age
thresholds, and for young-driver phrasing. Zero matches other than incidental
uses of *έτος* meaning a policy year.

**There is also no minimum licence-holding period**, which `DRIVER-AGE-MARKET.md`
§6 flagged as a gap: almost every competitor requires the licence to have been
held a year, commonly as an insurance condition. This certificate imposes none.

**What that does and does not license us to conclude.** It means no age or
tenure restriction is stated on the face of the policy. It does **not** mean
none exists — §2.2 hands that question to the booklet. Until the booklet is
read, the honest position is *unknown*, not *none*.

## 2. Three findings not asked about, in descending order of cost

### 2.1 There is no own-damage cover on this vehicle

The covered perils, in full:

| Cover | Sum insured | Deductible | Net premium |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Third-party bodily injury, per victim | €1,300,000 | — | €13.52 |
| Third-party property damage, per event | €1,300,000 | — | €25.10 |
| Driver personal accident | €5,000 | — | €0.50 |
| Damage caused by an uninsured vehicle | €6,000 | — | €0.89 |
| Legal advice | €600 | — | €0.18 |
| Legal protection | €3,000 | — | €2.13 |
| Accident care (Φροντίδα Ατυχήματος) | in force | — | €1.52 |
| Roadside assistance (ENOIKIAZOMENA) | in force | — | €10.11 |

The package is labelled **BRONZE RENTAL**. Total payable €82.76.

**Absent: own damage / collision (ίδιες ζημιές), theft (κλοπή), fire (πυρός),
and glass breakage.** Glass is mentioned only in a conditional paragraph —
"the glass cover, *if included in the policy*, activates 30 days after
inception" — and it is not in the schedule, so it is not included.

This is a **liability-only policy**. If this car is damaged in a collision the
renter causes, the policy pays the other party and nothing toward our own
vehicle.

**Why that is a commercial problem.** We sell **Full Damage Waiver at €12 a
day**, the single largest extra on the rate card. A damage waiver is a promise
that the customer will not be charged for damage to our vehicle. If no
own-damage insurance sits behind it, that promise is **self-insured** — every
FDW sale is Anadyon taking the risk onto its own balance sheet at €12 a day,
with the full repair cost as the exposure.

**Unverified, and it must be checked before it is acted on:** this is one
vehicle's certificate. There may be a separate own-damage or fleet policy that
was not in the file. The question for the broker is narrow and answerable in one
line: *is there own-damage cover on the fleet, and if so under which policy?*

### 2.2 The policy expires on 4 October 2026

Inception 04/07/2026 13:36, expiry 04/10/2026 23:59 — a **three-month** term,
about a month from the date this was read. §1 states there is **no automatic
renewal**: cover restarts only when the next period's premium is paid, and §1
also records that under Law 4261/2014 art. 169 §1 **cover begins only on payment
of the whole premium**.

A rental fleet that runs a day uninsured is not a paperwork problem.

### 2.3 Roadside assistance does not cover 50cc scooters

The assistance clause limits towing and roadside assistance to vehicles up to
3.5 tonnes and 3 metres in height, without dual rear wheels, **and to
motorcycles over 50cc**.

A 50cc scooter is therefore outside it. If the site, the terms or the rental
agreement promise roadside assistance without qualification, that promise is
wrong for the cheapest and most-rented category on the fleet. Worth reading our
own published wording against this before a customer is stranded and told so.

## 3. One structural question for the broker

The certificate has named-driver slots. **Οδηγός 1** is filled in (born
16/12/1974, licence issued 13/05/1998); **Οδηγός 2** and **Οδηγός 3** are blank.

On a rental policy this is normally a formality and any authorised renter is
covered — the package is called BRONZE **RENTAL** and the assistance line is
tagged **(ENOIKIAZOMENA)**, "rented", so it is plainly written for fleet use.

But it is worth one explicit confirmation, because the downside is total: if the
policy genuinely restricts cover to named drivers, then **no rental is covered
at all**, which would outrank everything else in this document.

## 4. What this changes

**For the under-23 insurance surcharge:** nothing. It is a commercial charge and
stands on its own. But note what cannot yet be claimed — this certificate shows
no insurer loading for young drivers, so the surcharge is **not evidenced as a
pass-through of an insurer's own charge**. Internal copy should not describe it
as one until the booklet or the broker confirms it.

**For the age limits in `DRIVER-AGE-MARKET.md` §7:** the recommendation there was
to lower motorbike minimums to the licence categories the law already sets, and
consider 19–20 for cars, both "subject to the insurer's answers". This document
does not block either — it simply does not address them. The booklet decides.

**The questions in `DRIVER-AGE-MARKET.md` §5 are now shorter**, because two are
partly answered and three new ones matter more:

1. Send the **Βιβλίο Όρων Ασφάλισης** — the terms booklet. Everything about age
   and licence tenure is in it and nothing can be settled without it.
2. **Is there own-damage cover on the fleet?** Under which policy? (§2.1)
3. **Are renters covered as unnamed drivers?** (§3)
4. Is the 4 October expiry being renewed, and on the same terms? (§2.2)
5. If we lower the motorbike age to the licence category (A1 at 18, AM at 16),
   does cover follow the licence or a flat age?
