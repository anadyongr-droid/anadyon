How to use this page. Article 15 provides that the Greek version prevails, so the Greek column is the operative text. It is deliberately not reproduced here: it must be carried over verbatim from the current printed form, or redrafted by counsel — not re-typed from a photograph and not machine-translated. The English below is the transcription in TRANSCRIPTION.md, with the corrections marked. See docs/CONTRACT-VS-WEBSITE.md for why articles 8 and 16 need redrafting before this goes to print.

1. Under this agreement ANADYON I.K.E., hereinafter called the Lessor, rents to the signatory Lessee the Vehicle including accessories and equipment described in this document, under the following terms and provisions as well as those inscribed on the front page of this document, which the Lessee fully accepts.

2. DURATION. The Lessor leases the Vehicle for the period indicated in this document. The minimum duration is 24 hours, and charges are based on 24-hour increments from the time of pick-up. The Lessor reserves the right to charge the Lessee for every additional day according to the relevant price list.

3. RENT. The Vehicle's rent is stated on the front page of this document. The rent is only changed in case adjustments are made by the relevant authorities to any of the items included in it (for example insurance rates, taxes).

4. EXPENSES, COSTS AND FEES. The Lessee shall bear: a) the rent and any community or other charges and premium airport surcharges; b) the charges for any additional services described in this document; c) fuel expenses including the value of missing fuel and the “fuel service” charge per the Price List at the time of the rental; d) any charge for loss or damage resulting from breach of the relevant articles of this agreement; e) all fines and processing fees for violations of traffic laws, and where the Lessee fails to pay fines, an “administrative processing service” charge per the official Price List; f) all costs, expenses or amounts of liability as described in Article 8 (Insurance Coverage), repair or replacement of the Vehicle in case of damage or theft, together with the non-refundable “damage processing service” charge specified in the current official price list; g) any damage resulting from violation of the Greek Traffic Law; h) VAT and any other taxes levied on the above.

5. DELIVERY – RECEIPT AND RETURN. The Vehicle is delivered to the Lessee in the condition described on the front page. The Lessee has inspected the Vehicle and received it without reservation. Signing this document constitutes delivery and receipt. On expiry the Lessee must return the Vehicle with the registration permit and other documents, in the same condition, at the place and time indicated on the front page.

6. TERMS OF USE. 6.1 The Vehicle cannot be used: a) by any person other than the persons signing this agreement as Lessees, who must hold a valid driving licence recognised by the Greek authorities held for at least one year prior to the lease, and be over 21 years of age for car rentals and over 18 years of age for motorbike rentals, and in every case hold the licence category the law requires for the vehicle ridden or driven; b) for illegal purposes or in violation of Greek law; c) in violation of the Greek Traffic Code; d) for the transfer of passengers or goods for a fee; e) to move or tow another vehicle; f) in races or on unpaved roadways; g) to sublet to third parties; h) to carry heavy, flammable, dirty or malodorous objects, weapons, or anything whose possession or transfer is prohibited or endangers the Vehicle, its passengers or third parties; i) outside Greece without the Lessor's prior consent — transfer by boat is prohibited without written consent; j) while the driver is under the influence of alcohol, drugs or any substance affecting driving ability; k) in violation of driving, customs or other regulations; l) to give driving lessons; m) in violation of any term of this document.

6.2 The Lessee and authorised drivers must: a) take necessary precautions against theft of the Vehicle; b) use the correct type of fuel; c) not allow a third party to perform works on the Vehicle without the Lessor's written consent; d) inform the Lessor immediately of any defect or problem.

7. ACCIDENTS. In case of accident or any other event (fire, theft, loss), the Lessee agrees: a) to notify the police immediately; b) to note the names and addresses of witnesses and others involved; c) to refrain from yielding to demands from third parties; d) to contact the Lessor; e) to collect all relevant information and evidence, including photographs, and send it to the Lessor; f) to complete and sign the relevant accident declaration form.

9. SUBSTITUTION RIGHT. The Lessor may, by written statement, indicate any natural or legal person to substitute for him in part or in whole in the rights and obligations under this agreement. The Lessee may not cede any rights or responsibilities under it.

10. OWNERSHIP. Full ownership and possession of the Vehicle belongs to the Lessor throughout the lease. This document is strictly and solely a leasing agreement; the Lessee acquires no rights other than those stated in it, may not cede or transfer any rights in the Vehicle, and is in no way a representative of the Lessor.

11. PROPERTY. The Lessee relieves the Lessor of responsibility for loss of or damage to any property placed in or on the Vehicle.

12. VIOLATION OF THE TERMS. 12.1 The Lessee and authorised drivers are jointly and fully responsible for upholding the terms of this document. 12.2 Where they violate or attempt to violate any obligation, or where information they provide is inaccurate, the Lessor may insist on performance or give notice of termination, demanding full restitution of actual damage and loss of future gains and payment of any arrears; on termination all dues become payable de jure. 12.3 Where the Lessee terminates early, all obligations remain due; prepaid rent for the remaining period is retained by the Lessor as a fair and reasonable penal clause. 12.4 The agreement terminates automatically on the death or legal restraint of a natural-person Lessee, or the dissolution, liquidation, bankruptcy or seizure of assets of a legal-person Lessee.

13. NON-WAIVING. Failure or delay by the Lessor in exercising rights under this document does not constitute and may not be taken as a waiver of them.

14. GENERAL PROVISIONS. (a) All terms are substantial; breach of any one brings the consequences in article 12. This document is the full and exclusive agreement between the parties and supersedes any contrary written or verbal agreement. Article headings are for convenience and cannot be used to interpret it. (b) Where there is more than one Lessee, all are responsible to the Lessor as a whole; default, protest, violation, any court decision, or any judicial or extrajudicial statement addressed to one applies de jure to the others.

15. JURISDICTION. Any difference arising from this agreement, including as to its interpretation, force or execution, falls under the jurisdiction of the Zakynthos Courts. In case of doubt as to interpretation, the Greek version of this agreement prevails.
