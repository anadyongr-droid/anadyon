## Two findings I got wrong, and corrected

Both are recorded because the reasoning error is the reusable part.

**1. The SPF hard-fail finding was wrong.** `EMAIL-DELIVERABILITY.md` had said
since 11 September that every booking confirmation hard-fails SPF. That
conflates the `From:` header with the envelope sender — **SPF is evaluated
against the Return-Path**. Tasos supplied full headers and they settled it:
envelope is `send.anadyon.gr`, SPF passes, DKIM passes, DMARC passes. E4 closed.
The proposed fix would have authorised the entire shared Amazon SES pool to send
as `anadyon.gr`, for nothing.

**2. I reported `web.archive.org` as blocked by policy.** It was not. I had
called it over plain `http://`, which the egress proxy does not carry. The
`Blocked by egress policy` response was about the scheme. Cost about an hour.

Both are the same mistake: reasoning from structure where only a measurement
answers. Worth remembering before the next confident inference.

## Where DMARC (E9) actually stands

`scripts/read-dmarc.mjs` + `npm run check:dmarc`, validated against three real
reports in `docs/dmarc/`. Tests in `lib/dmarcReader.test.ts` were confirmed to
fail when the parser was changed to read `auth_results` instead of
`policy_evaluated`.

**Answer: do not tighten `p=none` yet.** Four of five sources are grserver's
relay pool and all pass. The fifth — `mail-sor-f41.google.com` — is a Gmail
forwarder and **failed DMARC outright**: SPF passed for `gmail.com` (unaligned)
and no DKIM survived. Under `p=quarantine` that message goes to spam. Detail and
the suggested `pct=` ramp are in `EMAIL-DELIVERABILITY.md`.

**The reports were being deleted unread.** Twenty-plus sat in the Gmail bin from
five receivers. Gmail purges after 30 days, so August's are nearly gone. The
durable fix is pointing `rua=` at a parsing service — Postmark's is free, and
`rua` accepts a comma-separated list so the mailbox copy can stay.
