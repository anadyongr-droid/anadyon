## Proposed direction

- Zoho remains the authoritative operational mailbox.
- Incoming operational mail is retained in Zoho and forwarded to Gmail.
- Remote replies are sent from Zoho and copied to Gmail with a Zoho outgoing rule.
- A private office-only browser extension and isolated send relay are investigated for post-January-2027 corporate sending from the Gmail interface.
- The relay receives only a send-only Zoho OAuth grant, enforces allowed identities and rate limits server-side, and stores no message bodies or attachments.
- The existing booking-system Resend path stays separate.
