## What was decided, and why

- **The fourth survey pass changed the answer.** Three earlier passes concluded that no
  Zakynthos operator publishes motorbike prices over plain HTTP. That was wrong, and the
  reason is worth keeping: the earlier sweeps checked operator *homepages* for a currency
  symbol. This site shows none there — its prices live on a `Tariffe` page behind a nav
  menu. **Asking whether a front page shows a price is not the same as asking whether a
  site publishes one.**
- **The parser was written against the real page, not an imagined one.** The test embeds
  markup copied verbatim from the live tariff, including both copies of each table — the
  page ships every table twice, season-major for wide screens and transposed for narrow,
  with no class to tell them apart. The parser discriminates on the header row, so the
  duplicate is dropped structurally rather than by taking whichever came first.
- **`pickBandDays` refuses rather than guesses.** Their bands are 1–3, 4–6 and 7+ days and
  our sampled durations (2, 5, 10) fall one inside each, so the mapping is exact today. If
  they reshape the bands the column is skipped and reported, instead of a price being
  stored against a duration it was never quoted for.
- **No October figure is stored**, because they publish none — low season is May, June and
  September, high is 1 July to 31 August. A gap is honest; an invented shoulder price is not.
- **BikesBooking was ruled out on robots, not on difficulty.** Its prices come from `/api/`,
  which its own `robots.txt` disallows.
- **Riderly stays rejected**, and that is now clearly Tasos's call rather than mine: it is
  reachable only by presenting as a browser, which two scrapers in this repo reject in
  writing. With a second source built, the exception is not needed.

## What was found to be already done, or already written

- The repo already had a guard — `lib/rateSourceFreshness.test.ts` — asserting that every
  registered source has an importer and vice versa. It **caught the new source immediately**
  and failed the suite until the registry was updated. Widened to match the slug as a
  quoted string, since the new module writes `source: SOURCE` from an exported constant that
  the route and registry also read; tying those together is stronger than repeating a
  literal, and the guard's purpose (registry ↔ importer agreement) is unchanged. Verified by
  removing the registry entry and watching it fail.
- `DURATIONS = [2, 5, 10]` and `durationBand()` already existed and already fit this
  operator's bands exactly — nothing new was needed to line the two up.
