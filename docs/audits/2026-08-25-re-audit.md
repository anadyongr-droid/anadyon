## Verified

**Security headers.** HSTS `max-age=63072000; includeSubDomains; preload`; a CSP
plus a stricter report-only policy measuring the cost of a nonce migration;
`nosniff`; `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN`; `frame-ancestors 'none'`;
Permissions-Policy closing camera, microphone and geolocation.

**SEO.** Seven `hrefLang` references (`en`, `el`, `x-default`), reciprocated on
Greek pages; canonical on the apex; sitemap 200 with 26 URLs; robots allowing
all but `/admin/`; LocalBusiness schema with address, geo and opening hours.

**Performance.** TTFB across five samples: 97, 88, 91, 135, 84 ms for a 35 KB
document. Page weight 22 requests / ~460 KB — 203 KB script, 170 KB images,
82 KB CSS. DOM interactive 232 ms.

**Mobile layout.** `scrollWidth` equals `clientWidth` at 375 px with zero
elements past the edge. The August overflow finding is resolved.

**Touch targets — open.** 4 of 33 interactive elements under 44×44 px:

```
a      "Learn more"           72 × 18
button "Manage preferences"  165 × 38
button "Accept all"           99 × 38
a      "FAQ"                  23 × 16
```

Both cookie-banner buttons are in that list, and every first-time visitor must
hit one before doing anything else.

**Dark mode.** Applies correctly. Card body text (`gray-400`, rgb 153 161 175)
on card surface (`gray-800`, rgb 30 41 57) = **5.64:1**, passing AA. A spot
check, not a sweep.

## Not measured, and why

**Core Web Vitals.** The automation browser pane runs with
`visibilityState: hidden`; browsers defer painting for hidden tabs, so every
paint metric it reports is unreliable. The first attempt returned a 4.3 s First
Contentful Paint landing *after* load completed at 2.3 s — impossible, and
discarded rather than reported. PageSpeed Insights would settle it but its
anonymous quota was exhausted. LCP, CLS and INP stand where August left them:
unknown.

**Design, content and legal.** Both need a human pass.

---

## Two false findings caught before reporting

Recorded because the method matters more than the result.

**Zero hreflang tags.** `grep -c` counts matching lines, not occurrences, and
React emits `hrefLang` camel-cased. Corrected count: seven.

**Ten dark-mode contrast failures.** An automated sweep returned 2.4–2.6:1 on
every card. A sweep that fails *everywhere* is evidence against the method
first. The cause was reading Tailwind's `lab()` colours through a canvas without
resetting `fillStyle` between samples, so each reading inherited the previous
colour. With a sentinel reset the method returns exactly 21:1 for white on black
and the real value is 5.64:1 — passing.
